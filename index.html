CTRL+FUTURE

Student-led Technology Event · Project Coordination

CTRL+FUTURE was a planned one-week online technology event designed to connect school and university students with industry professionals across AI, web development, app development, video editing, freelancing, personal branding, and entrepreneurship.

  • Event planning & strategy
  • Speaker outreach & coordination
  • Professional communication
  • Volunteer & team coordination
  • Promotional & social media planning
  • Registration & conversion strategy
  • Partner & external outreach
  • Managing event materials

Outcome: CTRL+FUTURE ultimately did not proceed due to insufficient registrations. The experience gave me practical lessons in audience targeting, marketing runway, conversion strategy, pricing, and event validation.

What I Learned

  • Validate demand earlier
  • Start marketing much earlier
  • Build distribution before finalizing execution
  • Clearly communicate the value proposition
  • Track conversion rather than relying only on reach
  • Build a stronger registration funnel
  • Secure distribution partners earlier
