CTRL+FUTURE

Student Tech Event · Coordination

A planned one‑week online event connecting students with industry pros across AI, web, app dev, video editing, freelancing, branding & entrepreneurship.

  • Event strategy & planning
  • Speaker outreach & coordination
  • Professional communication
  • Volunteer & team coordination
  • Promo & social media planning
  • Registration & conversion
  • Partner & external outreach
  • Managing event materials

Outcome: CTRL+FUTURE didn't proceed due to low registrations. Big lesson: validate demand early, start marketing sooner, and build distribution before execution.

What I Learned

  • Validate demand earlier
  • Start marketing much earlier
  • Build distribution before execution
  • Communicate value proposition clearly
  • Track conversion, not just reach
  • Build a stronger registration funnel
  • Secure distribution partners earlier
